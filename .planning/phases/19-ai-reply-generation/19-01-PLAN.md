    - Calls call_openai_reply_generation and writes one AiUsageLog row with request_type="reply_generation"

    - On OpenAITransientError or OpenAIPermanentError: writes AiUsageLog.status = FAILED with error_code and error_message, then re-raises the original exception
    - On network error (other Exception): same FAILED log path, re-raises
    - Never suppresses exceptions — caller (view) handles them
