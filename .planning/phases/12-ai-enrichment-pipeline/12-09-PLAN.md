<objective>
Stop sending comment-less reviews (rating-only Google Business Profile reviews) to OpenAI. Enrichment for these reviews must short-circuit, derive sentiment locally from star rating, write no AiUsageLog row, and otherwise look identical to a successful enrichment from the perspective of downstream code.

Purpose: eliminates wasted OpenAI cost + latency on reviews that have no text content for the LLM to analyze. Caught during Phase 13 UI work — reviews with empty `comment` were producing meaningless `neutral` sentiments and zero tags despite being billed.

Output: Tightened `enrich_review` service that branches on `comment.strip() == ""`, with full test coverage for the skip path.
</objective>
