Add the `canonical` self-referential ForeignKey to `ActionItem` and create the migration. This is the data foundation for the entire duplicate merge feature. When set on an item, it marks that item as a merged duplicate pointing to its primary (canonical) item. When null, the item is standalone or canonical.

Purpose: Enables the service layer (plan 18-02) to mark duplicates and the selector to filter them out of the list.
