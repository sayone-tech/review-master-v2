<objective>
Prove on real PostgreSQL that the two Phase 31 writers are race-safe: the reaper never double-refunds and never refunds a committed reservation when it races a late commit or another reaper, overlapping lapse sweeps write exactly one LAPSED row per balance, and a reservation straddling the month boundary settles before its balance lapses. Then close the phase with the mandatory subagent reviews and every local gate.

Output: four new `pg_concurrency` tests in the existing `backend-pg` job, a SUMMARY recording the auditor findings and gate results, and the refreshed 31-VALIDATION.md sign-off.

Decisions proven here: D-06 (row lock + marker under overlap), D-07 (held balances skipped), D-08 (re-lapse after refund), D-10 (CAS-only reaper under overlap). CQA-01's reaper-vs-late-commit promise is delivered by Task 1.

## Trust Boundaries

| Boundary | Description |
|----------|-------------|
