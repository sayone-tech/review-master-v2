<objective>
Ship the reservation reaper end to end: Celery Beat (every 10 minutes) -> thin task -> reaper service -> Phase 30's `refund_reservation` CAS -> ledger, balance and audit, then the D-08 re-lapse of touched past-month balances. Prove every edge (TTL boundary, re-run, lost CAS, batch cap, RuntimeError, observability without review text, the month straddle), and document both Phase 31 schedules for operators.

Purpose: CREN-03 is what keeps abandoned requests from stranding credits and blocking reviews with 409. The reaper composes primitives that already exist (`refund_reservation` from Phase 30, `lapse_balance` from Plan 31-01), so the slice is thin, but it is the second writer racing the commit path; the Postgres proof of that race is Plan 31-03.

Output: `apps/credits/selectors/reservations.py`, `apps/credits/services/reaper.py`, the reaper task in `apps/credits/tasks.py`, migration 0004 (Beat seed), one setting and one route, `age_reservation` test helper, `test_reaper.py`, additions to `test_tasks.py` and `test_settings.py`, and the CLAUDE.md and runbook updates.

Decisions implemented here: D-08, D-09, D-10, D-11, D-12 (reaper batch size), plus the D-04 schedule documentation. Claude's-discretion choices recorded here: the reaper is CAS-only with no skip_locked pre-select (Task 1), bare `@shared_task` with no autoretry (Task 1), and the reaper's Beat seed is its own migration 0004 so no PeriodicTask ever points at an unregistered task in an intermediate commit.
</objective>
