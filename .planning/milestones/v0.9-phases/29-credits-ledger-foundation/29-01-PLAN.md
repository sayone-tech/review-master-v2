<objective>
Tracer slice for Phase 29: the first credit operation on a shop in a UTC month creates exactly one
balance row, seeded from the organisation's monthly allocation, with one INITIAL_ALLOCATION ledger row
and one `shop.credits.allocated` AuditLog row. This holds sequentially on SQLite and under 8 concurrent
first uses on real PostgreSQL, run by a new `backend-pg` CI job.

Purpose: every later v0.9 phase depends on this slice. Phase 30 reserves and commits against the balance,
Phase 31 lapses it, and Phases 32/33 read it. Proving the org field, the full ledger schema, the period
seam, the lazy-creation service and the Postgres race harness end to end now catches a schema or locking
dead end after one commit, not five.

Output: `Organisation.monthly_credit_allocation` (own migration), the `apps/credits` app with full
`ShopCreditBalance` + `CreditTransaction` schema and constraints (one migration), `services/periods.py`,
`services/allocation.py::ensure_balance`, credits settings, factories, sequential tests, and the Postgres
CI infrastructure (`pg_concurrency` marker, root-conftest skip/guard hook, `config/settings/test_pg.py`,
`backend-pg` job, `make test-pg`, first-use race test).
</objective>
