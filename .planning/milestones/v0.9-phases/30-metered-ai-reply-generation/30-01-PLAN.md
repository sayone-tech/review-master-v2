<objective>
Lead the phase with one end-to-end tracer: an Org Admin or in-scope Staff user generating an AI reply is charged the review's credit cost through reserve (short row-locked transaction) -> OpenAI call outside any transaction -> commit, and the response carries `credits_charged` and `credits_remaining`. Purpose: every later plan (outcome matrix, HTTP contract, concurrency proofs) builds on this slice. Proving the whole path on the first commit catches an architectural dead end (for example the append-only ledger blocking the usage-log link, research finding F1) before ten layers are built on it.

Output: the D-14 CAS extension, `apps/credits/services/consumption.py`, the `generate_reply_with_credits` orchestrator, the view wired to it, the re-pointed Phase 19/20 view tests, and `apps/credits/tests/test_consumption.py`.
